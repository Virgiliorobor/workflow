## How I Communicate
- Direct Spanglish communication mixing English and Spanish naturally. Terse, action-oriented — states what he needs without preamble. High precision on domain terms (tariff fractions, e.firma, IMMEX, AEO) used without explanation. Responds well to single-fix answers; loses patience with hedged, multi-option responses. Expects Claude to pick the right path, not list alternatives.
